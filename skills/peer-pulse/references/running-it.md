# Running Peer Pulse well

## Starting

- Explain it once in a team meeting: why (to see the help managers miss), how
  long (one minute), what happens (you'll pass thanks on; you choose how you're
  named), and what doesn't (no scores, no reviews based on it).
- Try it for four weeks, then ask the team whether to keep going.

## Keeping it light

- Same day and time each week. One reminder at most.
- Thank people within a day or two of the answers coming in.
- Some weeks will be quiet. Never chase, and never comment on who didn't reply.

## What you'll learn

- People whose help keeps coming up: look closer with Spotlight.
- Help that crosses teams: thank the other team's people and tell their manager.
- Someone never mentioned for weeks: they may be heads-down, new, remote or
  doing work that isn't visible. That's a prompt to look for their
  contributions (and maybe to check in), never a judgement.

## Variations

- **Family or friends:** "Who made your week easier?" at Sunday dinner, with
  the answers turned into a family thank-you (ThankYou `--family`).
- **Cross-team:** run it across a group of teams, and thank partner teams in
  your Team Story.
