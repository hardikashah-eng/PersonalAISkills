# Kudos patterns

## The formula

**What they did** (specific) → **the difference it made** → **the quality it
shows** → "Thank you."

> Bea, you traced last week's retry storm to a missing jitter, fixed it, and
> added a load test so it can't quietly come back. On-call hasn't been paged
> for it since. That's the kind of care that makes everyone's nights better.
> Thank you.

## By kind of work

| Work | What to name | Example line |
|---|---|---|
| Pull request | the problem, the approach, the care (tests, docs, migration) | "You split the migration into three safe steps, so we could ship without downtime." |
| Code review | the catch, how it was said, what it taught | "Your review on #519 caught the race before prod, and explained it so clearly I learned something." |
| Incident | calm updates, the diagnosis, the follow-up | "Your 15-minute updates kept everyone calm, and the root cause was in the channel before the call ended." |
| On-call shift | quiet nights earned, handoff quality, runbook fixes | "You fixed two runbook steps during your shift, so the next person won't trip on them." |
| Design doc | clarity, trade-offs, who it unblocked | "The trade-offs table settled a month of debate in one meeting." |
| Docs, onboarding | who it helped | "Three new hires shipped in week one using your setup guide." |
| Mentoring, unblocking | the time given, the growth seen | "You paired with Chen for two afternoons; his first PR merged with no changes." |
| Glue work | the invisible coordination | "You kept the three teams in sync on the cutover. It looked easy because you made it so." |

## Words to skip

Generic: "great job", "awesome work", "crushed it", "killed it".
Labels: "rockstar", "ninja", "guru", "10x", "hero" (it suggests heroics are expected), "legend".
Backhanded: "finally", "for once", "surprisingly", "even you".
Pressure: "now do it again", "set the bar for everyone else".

## Fairness

- Credit everyone involved, including reviewers and the person who reported the bug.
- Rotate who you recognize. Quiet, remote and junior people are easy to miss.
- Recognize behaviour and growth, not only big launches.
- Don't let kudos substitute for pay, promotion or fixing an overload.

## How often

Small and soon beats big and late: within a day or two of the work. Aim for a
few a week as a leader; the daily nudge helps (`../../nudge/SKILL.md`).
