# The feedback model

**What's going well → what happened → what it affected → what to try next →
their view → check in.**

| Part | Good | Not this |
|---|---|---|
| Going well | "Your cache design doc was the clearest we've had this year." | "You're great, but…" |
| What happened | "In the last two design reviews, the risks came after a 20-minute demo." | "Your reviews are disorganized." |
| What it affected | "Reviewers raised the risks in the last five minutes, and the decision slipped a week." | "People are frustrated with you." |
| What to try next | "Could you open with the decision you need and the top three risks, then demo?" | "Be more strategic." |
| Their view | "How did those reviews feel from your side?" | (no pause for their side) |
| Check in | "Let's look at how the next one goes." | (never mentioned again) |

## Wording that helps

- "I noticed…" / "In Tuesday's review…" (an observation, not a verdict)
- "The effect was…" / "What I saw happen next was…"
- "What would you try?" / "Would it help to…?"
- "I'm telling you because I want you to get the credit this work deserves."

## Examples

**To a direct report:**
> Your cache design doc was the clearest we've had this year. One thing to
> work on: in the last two design reviews, the risks came after a long demo,
> so reviewers raised them in the final minutes and the decision slipped a
> week. Next time, could you open with the decision you need and the top
> three risks, then demo? How did those reviews feel from your side?

**To a peer (ask first):**
> Can I share an observation from the incident call? When the fix was ready,
> it went to prod before the comms channel heard, so support learned about it
> from customers. Would you be up for posting a one-line heads-up before
> deploys during incidents? Happy to help write a template.

**Upward, to your manager:**
> Could I give you some feedback on Monday's planning? Priorities changed in
> the meeting, and the team left unsure which deadline mattered most. It
> would really help us if changes came with a quick written summary
> afterwards. Would that work for you?

## Positive feedback

Positive feedback uses the same shape (what happened, what it affected) and
goes through Kudos (`../../kudos/SKILL.md`), which also logs it as a strength.
