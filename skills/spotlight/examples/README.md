# Spotlight example

## q4-platform: a quarter of evidence for a fictional Platform team

- `evidence.jsonl`: 17 moments from GitHub, standup scans, Peer Pulse and the incident tool
- `analysis.md`: `spotlight.py analyze` shows Chen with a sustained pattern
  (seven weeks, four kinds of source, five colleagues), Bea and Dami as
  "worth watching", and a workload check for Chen (two on-call covers)
- `chen-impact-evidence.md`: the evidence `spotlight.py story` assembled
- `chen-impact-story.md`: the one-page story written from it
- `nomination.md`: an award nomination built from the story
- `note.json` and `outputs.txt`: the message to Chen (recognition, a stretch
  opportunity, and the workload question) and a note to his manager
