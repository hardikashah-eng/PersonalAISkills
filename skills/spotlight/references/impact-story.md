# Writing the impact story

## Shape (one page)

1. **The pattern** (two sentences): what they consistently do, and the
   difference it makes. "For the whole quarter, Chen has been the person who
   makes everyone else faster: reviews that catch real bugs, mentoring that got
   a new hire shipping in week one, and a test harness three teams now use."
2. **Three or four themes**, each with the two strongest examples and links:
   raising others, reliability, quality, knowledge sharing, across teams.
3. **What colleagues said**: two or three short quotes, credited, with their OK.
4. **The difference it made**: outcomes that are in the evidence (a rollout
   unblocked, an outage shortened, a team able to test something new).
5. **Beyond their role**: what they did that nobody asked them to.

## Rules

- Every claim traces to an evidence line. No adjectives without an example.
- Numbers only if they're in the source.
- Name the people they helped only if those people are fine with it.
- Keep workload concerns out of the story. They're a separate, private conversation.
- No comparisons with colleagues.
