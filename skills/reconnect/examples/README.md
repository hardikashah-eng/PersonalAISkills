# Reconnect — sample session

All people below are fictional.

## 1. Discovery: "Who have I lost touch with?"

> Here are a few people you were close to and haven't really talked with lately:
>
> 1. **Arjun** — teammate on the 2019 launch; you two did the Friday chai runs. Last real exchange: early 2025.
> 2. **Mei** — former colleague; she mentored you through your first year leading a team. Last real exchange: 2024.
> 3. **Daniel** — college project partner; you still have the robot-arm photos. Last real exchange: 2023.
>
> Anyone here you'd rather not reach out to? Pick one or more and I'll draft a note.

(Left off the list, without comment: a former colleague whose last thread was
a tense escalation, and a contact whose recent messages were condolences from
others.)

## 2. Arjun — WhatsApp, old teammate, shared-memory hook with a photo

See `arjun-whatsapp/` (message.json, postcard art, cleaned photo, rendered card).

Photo: found in the user's own photo library from the launch-week date range
(two chai glasses, no people), chosen by the user, location and camera data
stripped. `photo.jpg` here is an illustrated stand-in for a real photo.

**With the photo** (the one in message.json): send the photo first, then
> Hey Arjun, found this from launch week and it made me smile. I still think those Friday chai runs got us through that crunch. What are you building these days? Would love to hear, no need for a long reply.

**Without a photo**
> Hey Arjun, made chai at the office today and it was nowhere near as good as our Friday chai runs during the 2019 launch. I still think those runs got us through that crunch. What are you building these days? Would love to hear, no need for a long reply.

**Lighter variant**
> Hey Arjun, made chai at the office today and it was nowhere near Friday-chai-run standard. Hope you're doing well. What are you up to these days?

Why this hook: a small, happy, specific shared ritual; easy to smile at, easy
to answer. The photo gives him something to reply to straight away.

## 3. Mei — email, former mentor, Lunar New Year + "thought of you" hook

See `mei-lunar-new-year/`. Bilingual touch: greeting and closing in Chinese
(formal 您, since she was a mentor); body in English, the language they worked in.

Subject: **新年快乐, Mei**

Why this hook: a festival she celebrates gives a natural reason to write, and
a true "I still do what you taught me" line makes it personal.
