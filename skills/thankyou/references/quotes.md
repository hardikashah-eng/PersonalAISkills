# Opening quote

The quote frames the note. It should echo the *specific* thing the recipient did,
not gratitude in general.

## Rules

- **One quote, short** — ideally under 20 words, never more than 25.
- **Real and correctly attributed.** The internet is full of misattributed lines
  (many "Gandhi", "Einstein", "Buddha", "Lao Tzu", "Mother Teresa", "Shakespeare"
  quotes are fake). Use a line only if you are confident of the source
  (work, speech, letter). If not sure: label it "Proverb", use a different quote,
  or write an original line with no attribution. Never invent a quote and attach
  a real person's name.
- **Prefer public-domain or traditional sources**: classical authors, scripture
  and classical texts, folk proverbs, pre-1929 literature, government
  proclamations. Avoid song lyrics and modern poems entirely; avoid long passages
  from modern books.
- **Match the note's language** when a good native quote exists; give the
  original plus a one-line translation.
- **Respect the recipient's context.** Avoid religious text unless the user
  indicates it fits; secular or proverb options are the safe default.
- Nothing that could read as a backhanded message (e.g. quotes about forgiveness,
  failure, or aging).

## Match the theme to the moment

| The recipient… | Theme | Look for |
|---|---|---|
| mentored / taught | standing on shoulders, the teacher | Newton, Kabir, proverbs about teachers |
| helped in a crisis, at the right time | timely help | Thirukkural 102, Aesop |
| believed in the user / opened a door | kindness that multiplies | Aesop, proverbs |
| has been a long-time friend | friendship | Ichi-go ichi-e, proverbs |
| worked alongside the user | together | Helen Keller |
| general thank-you, Thanksgiving | gratitude itself | Cicero, Massieu, JFK proclamation, Shakespeare |
| Christmas / holidays | season | Dickens |

## Starter bank (well-attributed)

**English / classical**
- "If I have seen further it is by standing on the shoulders of Giants." — Isaac Newton, letter to Robert Hooke (1675)
- "No act of kindness, no matter how small, is ever wasted." — Aesop, *The Lion and the Mouse* (moral, common translation)
- "I can no other answer make but thanks, and thanks, and ever thanks." — William Shakespeare, *Twelfth Night*
- "Gratitude is not only the greatest of virtues, but the parent of all the others." — Cicero, *Pro Plancio* (translation)
- "Alone we can do so little; together we can do so much." — Helen Keller
- "As we express our gratitude, we must never forget that the highest appreciation is not to utter words, but to live by them." — John F. Kennedy, Thanksgiving Proclamation (1963)
- "I will honour Christmas in my heart, and try to keep it all the year." — Charles Dickens, *A Christmas Carol*

**Other languages** (original + translation)
- French — « La reconnaissance est la mémoire du cœur. » — Jean-Baptiste Massieu · *Gratitude is the memory of the heart.*
- Spanish — «Es de bien nacidos ser agradecidos.» — Refrán · *Being grateful is the mark of a noble heart.*
- Chinese — 饮水思源 (yǐn shuǐ sī yuán) — Proverb · *When you drink water, remember its source.*
- Japanese — 一期一会 (ichi-go ichi-e) — Tea-ceremony saying · *One time, one meeting — treasure every encounter.*
- Hindi — गुरु गोविंद दोऊ खड़े, काके लागूं पाय। बलिहारी गुरु आपनो, गोविंद दियो बताय॥ — Kabir · *Teacher and God both stand before me — whose feet do I touch first? The teacher's, for it was the teacher who showed me God.*
- Tamil — காலத்தி னாற்செய்த நன்றி சிறிதெனினும் ஞாலத்தின் மாணப் பெரிது — Thirukkural 102 · *Help given at the right moment, however small, is greater than the world.*
- Sanskrit — वसुधैव कुटुम्बकम् — Maha Upanishad · *The whole world is one family.*

When none fits, an original, unattributed line written for the moment is better
than a forced famous one, e.g. *"Some people open doors. A few walk through them with you."*
