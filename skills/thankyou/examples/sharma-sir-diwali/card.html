शुभ दीपावली · Happy Diwali

“गुरु गोविंद दोऊ खड़े, काके लागूं पाय। बलिहारी गुरु आपनो, गोविंद दियो बताय॥”

Teacher and God both stand before me. Whose feet do I touch first? The teacher's, for it was the teacher who showed me the way.

— संत कबीर (Kabir)

आदरणीय शर्मा सर, सादर प्रणाम।

In Class 9, when I could not make sense of my first computer program, you stayed back after school three days a week to sit beside me until it finally ran.

I write software for a living today, and I still hear your voice when I am stuck: 'Read the error slowly, beta. It is trying to help you.'

You gave your time without ever being asked, and you made a nervous boy believe he could learn anything.

May this Diwali fill your home with light, as you filled so many of our lives with it.

आपका आभारी शिष्य,

Rohan
