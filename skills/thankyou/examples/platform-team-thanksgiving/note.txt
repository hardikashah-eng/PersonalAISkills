To the Platform team,

In the spring we set ourselves a goal that sounded a little unreasonable: move every simulation run onto the new farm before the end of the year, without stopping anyone's work along the way.

We got there in October. Test runs that used to take a week now finish the same afternoon. That happened because of late nights nobody asked for, careful reviews, and one habit you kept no matter what: no one debugs alone.

Three other teams now run their tests on what you built, and I am carrying that same habit into every group I work with.

This Thanksgiving I am grateful for every one of you who touched this, on-site and remote.

With gratitude and pride,
Hardik
