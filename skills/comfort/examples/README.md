# Comfort examples

All people are fictional; the headers were drawn by the skill.

## priya-sympathy: a colleague's father has died
- Acknowledges plainly, names her dad, shares one warm memory.
- One specific offer (covering her reviews) and a food thought (dal and rice
  left at the door, "no need to come out").
- "No need to reply." No humour and no fun fact: it's a fresh loss.
- Opening quote: Helen Keller, *We Bereaved* (1929), shortened with an
  ellipsis as Quote Investigator documents.

## rohan-layoff: a close friend was laid off
- Doesn't name the layoff ("the changes at your company") or give advice.
- Affirms him, offers introductions when he's ready, and dinner on Friday.
- Because the sender knows Rohan would enjoy it, a light moment: *Seinfeld*,
  "The Serenity Now" (season 9, episode 3), named by episode for him to find on
  his streaming service, since no official clip link was confirmed.

```bash
python3 ../../thankyou/scripts/safety_check.py priya-sympathy/note.json --profile comfort
python3 ../../thankyou/scripts/render_card.py priya-sympathy/note.json --art priya-sympathy/art.svg --out /tmp/priya.html
```
