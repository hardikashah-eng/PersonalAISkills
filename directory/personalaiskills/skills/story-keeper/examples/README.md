# Story Keeper example

## nani-stories: "Nani's Stories", three chapters for Diwali
- Cover, dedication, three chapters (story, pull quote, picture), closing words.
- Chapter 1 has a voice player with the words heard in the clip.
- `keepsake-preview.jpg` shows the whole booklet.

Everything here is a stand-in: Nani and the stories are fictional, the
pictures are drawn pencil sketches (labelled as such, drawn as SVG and saved
here as PNG), and the voice clip is left out of this example. In real use the
photos are the family's own and the clip is the elder's real voice.

The booklet is built from `nani-stories/keepsake.json` as in
`../references/booklet-layout.md`, and checked with
`../../thankyou/references/safety-checklist.md` (every-note and family checks).
