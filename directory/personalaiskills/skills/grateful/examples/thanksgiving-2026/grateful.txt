“Gratitude is not only the greatest of virtues, but the parent of all the others.”
— Cicero

This Thanksgiving, I'm grateful for…

It was a year of small mercies, and I don't want them to slip by unnoticed.

✦ My sister — for the Sunday calls that never once felt like a chore. I came out of a heavy year steadier than I went in.
   ↪ I've started calling Dad every Sunday too.

✦ The people I get to work with — for building something hard together and still laughing at the end of it. They reminded me that good work is mostly good people.
   ↪ I'm keeping an hour every week to help a new engineer find their feet.

✦ Ordinary evenings at home — for dinners that run late and homework at the kitchen table. They're the part of the day I'd keep if I could keep only one.
   ↪ This winter we'll cook for our neighbour once a week.

May I notice these things more often, and pass a little of each one on.
Hardik, Thanksgiving 2026

P.S. Fun fact: Thanksgiving became a national holiday in 1863, after Sarah Josepha Hale spent 17 years writing letters asking for it. Gratitude, it turns out, takes persistence.
