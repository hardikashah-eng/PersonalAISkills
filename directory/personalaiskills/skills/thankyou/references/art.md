# One-of-one artwork

A small illustration drawn for this recipient alone. It should feel hand-made,
calm, and warm — a keepsake, not clip art.

## Spec

- SVG, `viewBox="0 0 320 200"`, flat shapes, 4–6 colours from the card palette.
- No external images, fonts, or scripts. Self-contained.
- Text in the art is optional; if used, only the recipient's first name or
  initial, or a single word ("thank you", "shukriya").

## Never draw

- Faces or likenesses of real people (abstract figures/silhouettes are fine).
- Logos, brands, company marks, products, mascots, or copyrighted characters.
- Anything that reveals PII, finances, health, or private events
  (no hospital, money, pills, house numbers, licence plates).
- Religious symbols unless the user indicates they fit.

## Compose from 3 layers

1. **Story motif (from the moment)** — the main element.
2. **Personal motif (from the recipient)** — a hobby, a place type, something
   they love, mentioned in their messages or by the user.
3. **Occasion motif** — framing or background.

Plus one **one-of-one detail**: their favourite colour as the accent, a small
constellation, a tiny first-name initial carved on a tree, the number of years
you've known each other as stars or leaves.

## Craft tips

- Simple geometry: circles, rounded rectangles, paths with gentle curves.
- Layering for depth: sky → far hills (lighter) → near hills (darker) → subject.
- Leave breathing room; the art sits above the quote.
- Use `opacity` for soft light; avoid gradients heavier than two stops.
- Check that it reads at thumbnail size.
