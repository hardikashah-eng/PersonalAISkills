# Safety checklist

Go through this list for every note, post, card, booklet and log entry before you
show it to the user. Read every visible field: quote, greeting, body, items,
closing, signature, photo caption and alt text, fun fact, light moment, subject,
short and channel versions, public post and manager note.

If you can run code in your environment, you may write a quick pattern scan for the
lists below to help, but the read-through is what counts. It is a backstop for
judgement, not a replacement: a clean pass does not prove a note is safe.

**Block** means fix it (abstract or remove) before the user sees the note.
**Review** means read it again and change it unless it's clearly fine.
Match terms as whole words or phrases, except where a list says it matches word starts.

## Every note

Block:

- **Personal details:** email addresses, phone numbers, web links, numbers of six or more
  digits (IDs, accounts), street addresses, and social handles (`@name`).
- **Money amounts:** a currency sign with a number (`$`, `€`, `£`, `¥`, `₹`, `₩`), or a number
  with `k`, `lakh`, `crore`, `USD`, `INR`, `EUR`, `GBP`, `dollars`, `rupees`, `euros`, `pounds`,
  `yuan` or `yen`.
- **Financial terms:** `salary`, `salaries`, `paycheck`, `loan`, `loaned`, `lend`, `lending`, `lent me`, `debt`, `debts`, `mortgage`, `rent money`, `bank account`, `bank loan`, `credit card`, `bonus`, `pay raise`, `a raise`, `compensation`, `stock options`, `investment`, `investments`, `bankrupt`, `was broke`, `were broke`, `flat broke`, `money`, `paid for`, `cash`, `wire transfer`, `venmo`, `paypal`, `upi`.
- **Health terms** (match word starts, so `diagnos` catches diagnosis): `hospital`, `surgery`, `surgeon`, `cancer`, `chemo`, `chemotherapy`, `tumor`, `diagnos`, `therapy`, `therapist`, `counseling`, `counselling`, `counselor`, `counsellor`, `psychiatr`, `medication`, `meds`, `pills`, `prescription`, `pregnan`, `miscarriage`, `ivf`, `fertility`, `depression`, `depressed`, `anxiety`, `panic attack`, `rehab`, `addiction`, `sober`, `illness`, `disease`, `injury`, `injured`, `icu`, `er visit`, `clinic`, `doctor`, `diabetes`, `heart attack`, `had a stroke`, `suffered a stroke`, `covid`, `disabled`, `disability`, `mental health`, `suicid`, `self-harm`, `eating disorder`.
- **Other sensitive topics** (word starts): `lawsuit`, `court case`, `arrest`, `visa status`, `green card`, `immigration`, `divorce`, `laid off`, `got fired`, `was fired`, `were fired`, `layoff`.
- **The recipient's own topics to avoid:** anything the user said to steer clear of.

Review:

- **Harsh or judgemental:** `stupid`, `idiot`, `dumb`, `lazy`, `useless`, `pathetic`, `annoying`, `hate`, `shut up`, `crazy`, `insane`, `loser`, `weird`, `ugly`, `fat`, `old man`, `old lady`, `for once`, `even you`, `despite your`, `unlike you`, `for a change`, `you never`, `you always`, `should have`, `finally did`, `about time`, `damn`, `hell`, `crap`.
- **Clichés:** `words cannot express`, `words can't express`, `from the bottom of my heart`, `you're the best ever`, `best ever`, `means the world`.
- More than one `!` in the whole note.
- A body over about 220 words.

## Photos, sketches and stock images

When the picture is a drawn sketch or a stock photo, block any wording that presents it as
a real photo of the moment: `found this photo`, `found this picture`, `found this from`, `found this today`, `look what turned up`, `remember this`, `photo of us`, `picture of us`, `this photo of`, `this is us`, `here's us`, `us at the`, `photo from`, `picture from`, `snapped this`, `took this`. A stock photo needs its licence
(and credit, if the licence asks) recorded.

## Groups

If the note names any member of the group, it must name every member (block). Otherwise
it names no one.

## Extra checks by kind of note

Use the profile that fits, in addition to the checks above.

### Reconnect check-ins

- Block, guilt / time-counting: `it's been forever`, `it has been forever`, `been ages`, `been so long`, `long time no`, `years already`, `sorry i disappeared`, `sorry i've been mia`, `sorry for being mia`, `i'm terrible at`, `i'm the worst at`, `i am terrible at`, `you probably don't remember`, `you may not remember`, `dropped the ball`.
- Block, surveillance-sounding: `saw on your profile`, `noticed on your profile`, `noticed you moved`, `saw you moved`, `noticed you were at`, `saw you were at`, `saw you were in`, `checked in at`, `your location`, `stalking`.
- Review, possible hidden ask: `quick favor`, `quick favour`, `refer me`, `referral`, `could you introduce`, `can you introduce`, `looking for a job`, `job search`, `are you hiring`, `pick your brain`.

### Team and org notes, tributes and kudos

- Block, confidential business detail: `confidential`, `internal only`, `do not share`, `don't share`, `revenue`, `headcount`, `profit`, `margin`, `arr`, `burn rate`, `valuation`.
- Review, reads like a performance review: `exceeded expectations`, `meets expectations`, `met expectations`, `performance review`, `performance rating`, `rating`, `promotion`, `promoted`, `top performer`, `high performer`, `low performer`, `pip`.
- Review, ranking people: `mvp`, `most valuable`, `the best on the team`, `best of the team`, `star of the team`.
- Review, non-inclusive or cliché wording: `guys`, `rockstar`, `rockstars`, `rock star`, `rock stars`, `ninja`, `ninjas`, `10x`, `manpower`.

### Family notes

- Block, comparison or ranking: `favorite child`, `favourite child`, `favorite sibling`, `favourite sibling`, `favorite grandchild`, `favourite grandchild`, `unlike your`, `better than your`, `why can't you`.
- Block, pressure: `when are you getting`, `when will you`, `settle down`, `give us grandchildren`, `grandkids yet`, `it's time you`.
- Review, guilt or debt language: `after all you sacrificed`, `after all you've sacrificed`, `after everything you sacrificed`, `can never repay`, `can't ever repay`, `i owe you everything`, `i don't call enough`, `i should call more`, `i know i never`.

### Gratitude lists

- Block, comparison with others' misfortune: `at least i'm not`, `at least i am not`, `at least we're not`, `at least we are not`, `unlike some people`, `unlike others`, `less fortunate`, `luckier than`, `better off than`, `poor people`, `those poor`.
- Review, bragging or humble-bragging: `humbled to announce`, `so humbled`, `blessed beyond measure`, `#blessed`, `living my best life`, `crushed it`, `killing it`, `promotion`, `promoted`.
- Review, guilt: `i don't deserve`, `i never thank`, `i should have been`, `i know i haven't`.

### Comfort messages

- Block, minimising or silver-lining: `at least`, `everything happens for a reason`, `happens for a reason`, `better place`, `god's plan`, `part of god's plan`, `silver lining`, `look on the bright side`, `could have been worse`, `could be worse`, `it was meant to be`, `time heals`, `move on`, `moving on`, `get over it`, `you'll get over`, `plenty of fish`.
- Block, making it about the sender: `i know exactly how you feel`, `i know how you feel`, `when my`, `same thing happened to me`.
- Block, pressure or advice: `stay strong`, `be strong`, `you should`, `you need to`, `have you tried`, `you must`, `don't cry`, `cheer up`, `let me know if you need anything`.
- Review, check the tone: `fun fact`, `lol`, `haha`, `!!`.
- Block: no fun fact in a hard-time note.
- Review: a light moment only if the user confirmed humour is welcome, never for a fresh loss.

### Public posts (LinkedIn and others)

- Block, confidential or unreleased: `confidential`, `internal only`, `nda`, `under nda`, `unreleased`, `not yet announced`, `unannounced`, `embargo`, `embargoed`, `stealth`, `codename`, `code name`, `pre-release`.
- Block, security details: `vulnerability`, `vulnerabilities`, `exploit`, `cve`, `security incident`, `breach`, `data leak`, `leaked`, `credentials`, `password`, `api key`.
- Block, people matters: `performance review`, `pip`, `headcount`, `layoff`, `layoffs`, `reorg`, `promotion packet`, `compensation`, `salary`, `rating`.
- Review, may be internal (check it's public): `incident`, `outage`, `sev1`, `sev2`, `sev-1`, `sev-2`, `postmortem`, `post-mortem`, `customer`, `customers`, `roadmap`, `launch date`, `beta`, `internal`, `on-call`, `oncall`, `rollback`, `downtime`, `pager`, `escalation`, `deadline`.
- Block: ticket or PR references (`#482`, `ABC-123`) and internal links (intranet, Jira,
  Confluence, Slack, Teams, SharePoint, Lark/Feishu, Grafana, Datadog, PagerDuty, private IPs).
- Review: metrics and numbers (`40%`, `3x`, `200 ms`, `10k users`): check they're public.
- Block until the user confirms the colleague is happy to be named publicly.
- Review until the user confirms the post fits their company's social media policy.
- Check only the public post with this profile; private versions use the checks above.

### Feedback and growth-log entries

- Block, labels the person instead of the behaviour: `lazy`, `careless`, `incompetent`, `sloppy`, `attitude problem`, `bad attitude`, `not a team player`, `unprofessional`, `lack of commitment`, `doesn't care`, `don't care`, `useless`, `hopeless`, `immature`.
- Block, absolutes: `you always`, `you never`, `every single time`, `constantly`.
- Block, comparing to others: `unlike`, `why can't you be like`, `compared to`.
- Review, coded or vague words (describe the behaviour instead): `abrasive`, `bossy`, `emotional`, `aggressive`, `shrill`, `too nice`, `culture fit`, `not a fit`, `attitude`, `personality`, `lack of polish`, `executive presence`, `too quiet`, `too direct`.
- Review, softeners that hide the point: `just a thought`, `no big deal`, `maybe it's just me`, `sort of`, `kind of`.
- Review: each piece of feedback needs what happened, what it affected and what to try next.

### Apologies and follow-ups

- Block, non-apology: `sorry if`, `sorry you feel`, `sorry that you feel`, `if anyone was offended`, `if i offended`, `mistakes were made`, `i'm sorry but`, `sorry, but`, `no offense`, `you misunderstood`, `you took it the wrong way`, `to be fair`, `you overreacted`, `calm down`.
- Review, excuses that dilute it: `but`, `however`, `i was just`, `i only`, `anyway`.
- Review: the note says plainly, in the first person, what the sender is sorry for.
- Review: the note says what the sender will do differently.

## Introductions

Block an introduction until both people have said yes privately (double opt-in).

## Fun facts and light moments

- Review: a fun fact needs a record of where it was verified, and stays under about 35 words.
- Review: a light moment needs its official or public-domain source, and a kind tone.

## Then read it as the recipient

Would they feel seen and happy? Would anything make them uncomfortable if someone read it
over their shoulder? Does anything touch a trigger, or break a norm of their culture or the
relationship? Would they be glad to see this photo?
