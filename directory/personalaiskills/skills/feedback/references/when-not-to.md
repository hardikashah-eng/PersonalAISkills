# When feedback isn't the right tool

Stop and suggest other support when the situation involves:

- **Possible health, wellbeing or personal-life causes** (sudden change in
  performance, signs of burnout or distress): start with a caring check-in,
  not feedback, and point to your company's employee assistance program or HR.
- **Harassment, discrimination, safety or legal concerns:** follow your
  company's reporting process and involve HR. Don't write it up as feedback.
- **A formal performance process** (a performance plan, a warning): HR and
  your company's templates lead; this skill can help you prepare talking
  points, never the formal document.
- **Feedback you're asked to deliver for someone else:** encourage them to
  give it directly, or deliver only what you've seen yourself.
