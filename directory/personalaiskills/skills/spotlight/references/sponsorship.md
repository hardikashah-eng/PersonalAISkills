# Elevating someone: sponsorship

Recognition says "thank you". Sponsorship changes what happens next for the
person: the opportunities they get, who knows their work, and how their
contributions count at review time.

## Options, from light to strong

| Step | When it fits | Skill that helps |
|---|---|---|
| A specific thank-you | always | Kudos |
| A note to their manager and skip-level | the user isn't their manager, or wants leadership to know | Kudos (`manager_note`) |
| A mention at all-hands or in the Team Story | the work helped many people | Team Story |
| An introduction to a senior leader or expert | it would open a door they want | Intro |
| A stretch opportunity | they've said they want to grow in that direction | (a conversation) |
| An award nomination | the company has one that fits | this skill |
| Evidence for a promotion case | they're working at the next level | this skill and the growth log |
| A public shout-out | the work is public, and they agree | Kudos (`public_post`) |

## A nomination (when there's no company template)

- **Who and what for** (one line)
- **The pattern** (two sentences)
- **Three examples**, each: what they did → the difference it made → link
- **What colleagues say** (two short quotes, with their OK)
- **Why now**

Keep to one page. Follow the company's template, criteria and word limits when there are any.

## The workload conversation

If Spotlight flagged workload, have this talk alongside the recognition, not instead of it:

> "I've noticed you've covered on-call twice this month and jumped into the
> outage off-rota. I really appreciate it, and I also want to make sure it's
> sustainable. How's the load feeling? Is there anything I should take off
> your plate or spread around?"

Then act: rebalance the rota, spread reviews, make the help part of their
official scope (and credit it), or protect focus time.

## Follow through

Log each step as `--type sponsorship` in the growth log and update it with
the outcome. Sponsorship that never produces an outcome is worth a second look.
