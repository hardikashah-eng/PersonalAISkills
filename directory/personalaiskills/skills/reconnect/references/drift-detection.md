# Finding drifted connections

Goal: a short list (3–5) of people the user would be *glad* to hear back from,
found with as little reading as possible. Read-only, always.

## Signals

**Warm past** (any of):
- Real two-way exchanges over months — not one-off threads.
- Personal content mixed in with work (jokes, life updates, "how's your family").
- Gratitude in either direction ("thank you", "you saved me", 🙏).
- Shared projects, trips, teams, classes, or recurring 1:1s.
- They wrote the user a recommendation, or the user wrote one for them.

**Quiet present**:
- No meaningful exchange in 6+ months (12+ for old friends).
- Only automated or group traffic since (birthday reactions, group threads).

## Where to look

| Source | What to scan |
|---|---|
| Email | Correspondents by thread count over past years vs. last 6–12 months; skip lists, receipts, vendors, recruiters |
| Slack / Teams / Lark | DMs that were active and went quiet; people who left the company (they're often the easiest to lose) |
| LinkedIn | Former colleagues; people who wrote recommendations; old message threads |
| Calendar | Recurring 1:1s or mentoring series that ended |
| WhatsApp / SMS *(opt-in)* | 1:1 chats that were active and went quiet — never scan groups unless the user points to one |

Search by counts and dates first; open message content only for the shortlist,
and only enough to find a hook.

## Leave alone — do not suggest

Exclude anyone with these signals, and don't mention why:
- Conflict or tension in the last exchanges (arguments, cold replies, HR or legal context).
- The user or the other person said they wanted space, unfollowed, blocked, or muted.
- Former romantic partners.
- Estranged family, unless the user specifically asks.
- Possible bereavement: condolence messages *about* this person, memorial notices,
  or an account that went silent alongside such messages. Never suggest someone
  who may have passed away.
- The last exchange was about a hard event for them (illness, loss, layoff) and
  went unanswered — the user may want to approach that differently; ask
  privately rather than listing them.
- Purely transactional relationships (landlord, vendor, recruiter) unless the user asks.

When unsure, leave them off. A missed suggestion costs nothing; a painful one does.

## Scoring (0–2 each, keep the top 3–5)

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warmth | polite/transactional | friendly | personal, affectionate |
| Depth | one shared thread | shared project or period | long shared history |
| Mutuality | one-sided | mostly mutual | clearly mutual |
| Hook available | none | season/question only | real shared memory or public milestone |
| Drift | < 6 months | 6–18 months | 18+ months but was close |

Prefer people where the user was helped or helped them (gratitude both ways
makes reconnection easy), and people who changed companies or cities — the
classic "we just lost touch" cases.

## Summarise for the user

One line per person: first name, how they're connected, one warm hook, rough
"last real exchange" (season/year, not exact date). No message quotes, no
sensitive detail, no contact info. Let the user choose.
